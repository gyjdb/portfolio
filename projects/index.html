01Named Entity Recognition in Chinese Fiction

01 / NLP research

Named Entity Recognition in Chinese Fiction

Independent research

Sep 2026 – Oct 2026

Python, PyTorch, Chinese RoBERTa

A six-type named entity recognition study on a long Chinese web novel with many author-invented names.

Annotation
Defined annotation guidelines and adjudication procedures for characters, locations, organizations, and other fictional entities.
Training
Evaluated a two-stage Chinese RoBERTa tagger using 749 weakly labeled chapters followed by 100 LLM teacher-labeled chapters, and compared labeling and training choices across six rounds of controlled experiments.
Result
Reached 90.8% strict entity-level F1 (three-seed mean) on a held-out, 40-chapter silver-standard test set, 2.0 percentage points above the teacher-only baseline.

02Intraday Crypto Prediction and Time-Series Evaluation

02 / Time-series research

Intraday Crypto Prediction and Time-Series Evaluation

Aug 2025 – Nov 2025

Python, LightGBM, Logistic Regression

Minute-level prediction for five crypto assets, evaluated with chronological splits and trading-cost-aware backtests.

Pipeline
Developed a minute-level research pipeline for BTC, ETH, BNB, XRP, and SOL at 1-, 5-, and 15-minute horizons.
Preprocessing & labels
Applied rolling time-of-day normalization and path-dependent triple-barrier labels.
Evaluation
Compared LightGBM with logistic regression using chronological 2023/2024/2025 train/validation/test splits, and evaluated transaction costs and turnover sensitivity with non-overlapping backtests.
